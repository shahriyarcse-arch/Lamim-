13 · Questions, answered

Nothing hidden behind the calm.

What is included in Lamim?

Salah, Nafl, Dhikr, Mujahid, Gym, Career, Goals and Sunnah, Smart Finance, Savings Vaults, Analysis, Spiritual Health Score, Profile, local backups, and PWA installation.

Does Lamim need an account?

No. Lamim works without email, password, phone number, or a central user database. Your personal reflections stay exclusively on your device.

Can I use it without internet?

Yes. The app is built for offline use with local storage and service-worker caching. All calculations happen on-device.
